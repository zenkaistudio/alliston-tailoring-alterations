How It Works

1

Book or Walk In

Send us your request below or simply walk into the studio during business hours. No appointment necessary for most alterations.

2

Before You Come In

What to
bring

  • ✦
    Your Garment The item you'd like altered — cleaned if possible.
  • ✦
    Proper Shoes The exact shoes you plan to wear — especially important for hem work.
  • ✦
    Shapewear / Undergarments For bridal and formal gowns, wear what you'll wear on the day.
  • ✦
    Inspiration (optional) Photos or references for custom or reconstruction work are always welcome.
